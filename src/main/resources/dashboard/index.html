What the data can tell us

Sales and cancellations from one UK retailer. No subscription renewals, app-store receivables, settlement delays, or confirmed fraud labels.

The lab converts GBP to simulated USD at a fixed 1.25 USD/GBP and maps the selected historical day onto a fresh demo reporting period. This is an illustrative transformation, not historical FX or an actual financing decision.

The velocity baseline uses only the preceding seven observed days. Missing calendar dates are not filled. Low history is visible; it is not silently treated as a reliable baseline.

IMPLEMENTED · DURABLE INTENT

The invariants we defend

  • One request identity binds one immutable payment command.
  • Unknown bank outcomes keep their reservations.
  • A hold prevents recovery from submitting a new bank request.
  • Repayment never recycles already-funded earnings.
  • Every posted journal balances; history is append-only.
  • A discrepancy opens an exception, not an invented adjustment.

Where this prototype stops

The broader architecture documents real provider integrations, fraud models, returns, multi-currency, partial and aggregated collections, residual payouts, authentication, and deployment controls. Those are not implemented here.

The dashboard's worker-stop scenario interrupts execution in-process. The integration suite separately tests an actual child-JVM halt. Opening balances are fixtures, not a full opening general ledger.
